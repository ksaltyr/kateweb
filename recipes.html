chocolate chip cookies

ingredients

  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 1/4 cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • chocolate chips

note: better slightly underbaked

chocolate chip cookies

instructions

  1. Brown the butter and let it cool.
  2. Mix the butter with both sugars.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla.
  4. Mix in the dry ingredients.
  5. Fold in chocolate chips.
  6. Bake at 350°F for about 10–12 minutes.

take them out when they still look a little underdone

focaccia

ingredients

  • 4 cups flour
  • 2 tsp yeast
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups warm water
  • olive oil
  • rosemary
  • flaky salt

focaccia

instructions

  1. Mix flour, yeast, salt and water.
  2. Let the dough rise.
  3. Cover a pan generously with olive oil.
  4. Stretch the dough into the pan.
  5. Dimple aggressively.
  6. Add rosemary, salt and more olive oil.
  7. Bake until golden.

tomato soup

ingredients

  • tomatoes
  • onion
  • garlic
  • butter
  • vegetable or chicken stock
  • cream
  • salt + pepper

tomato soup

instructions

  1. Cook onion and garlic in butter.
  2. Add tomatoes and stock.
  3. Simmer.
  4. Blend until smooth.
  5. Add cream.
  6. Season until it tastes correct.
